Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-01

Freshers University Applicants ("we", "us") helps university students find their course groups and stay connected with relevant campus social activities. This policy explains what information we collect, how we collect it, why we use it, and how you can opt out of communications sent using the Meta WhatsApp Business Platform.

How we collect your information

  • We collect your information directly from you, when you voluntarily complete our physical registration pamphlet, distributed on campus to new students. A digital copy of this pamphlet is available here.
  • The pamphlet has a dedicated field asking for your WhatsApp number, alongside your full name, roll number, session, and course(s), so that we can add you to the correct course discussion group and keep you updated on relevant activities.
  • We only contact phone numbers submitted to us this way. We do not purchase, scrape, or otherwise obtain phone numbers from any other source.

Information we collect

  • Full name, roll number, session, and course(s), as provided on the registration pamphlet.
  • Your WhatsApp number, and the content and delivery status (sent, delivered, read, failed) of messages exchanged with you, including any replies you send us.
  • Basic account information for staff who use our internal dashboard to run these communications (name, email, login credentials) — separate from student data, and used solely to control staff access to the Service.

How we use this information

  • To add you to the WhatsApp group for your course and activities, and send you the group's join link.
  • To send you information relevant to your session, roll number, course, and official Freshers' Week activities and ticket links.
  • To respond to messages you send us, including through an assisted reply service overseen by our team.
  • To maintain delivery records and operate, secure, and improve the Service.

Your consent and how to opt out

Providing your WhatsApp number on the registration pamphlet is voluntary, and is the basis on which we contact you. You can stop receiving messages from us at any time by replying "STOP" or "unsubscribe", or by tapping the Unsubscribe button included in our messages — we action opt-outs immediately and will not message that number again. You can also contact us using the details below to ask what information we hold about you or to request it be deleted.

How we share information

Phone numbers and message content are shared only with Meta / WhatsApp as required to deliver messages through the WhatsApp Business Platform. We do not sell or rent your data to third parties, and we do not share it for advertising purposes.

Data retention

Contact and message data is retained for as long as needed to operate the Service and to maintain a record of past communications. You may request deletion of specific data by contacting us using the details below.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we hold, including access controls and encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS) for all data in transit.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy, want to know what information we hold about you, or wish to request that your data be deleted, contact us at jonny@ultimatefreshersevents.com or via WhatsApp at 07413 332016.

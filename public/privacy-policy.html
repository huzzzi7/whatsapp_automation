Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-01

This application ("the Service") sends WhatsApp messages to phone numbers you provide, using the Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API. This policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and how it is protected.

Information we collect

  • Phone numbers you upload for outbound campaign messaging.
  • Message content, message template names, and delivery status (sent, delivered, read, failed) returned by the WhatsApp Cloud API.
  • Basic account information for users of this internal tool (name, email, login credentials), used solely to control access to the Service.

How we use this information

  • To send WhatsApp template messages to the phone numbers you provide.
  • To track and display delivery status for messages sent through the Service.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the Service.

How we share information

Phone numbers and message content are shared only with Meta / WhatsApp as required to deliver messages through the WhatsApp Business Cloud API. We do not sell or rent your data to third parties, and we do not share it for advertising purposes.

Data retention

Campaign and contact data is retained for as long as needed to operate the Service and to maintain a record of past campaigns. You may request deletion of specific data by contacting us using the details below.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we hold, including access controls and encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS) for all data in transit.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or wish to request that your data be deleted, contact us at jonny@ultimatefreshersevents.com.
